HOW IT WORKS

A structured process, from booking to a happy home.

We follow the same five stages for every pet, every time — so nothing gets missed and everyone involved knows exactly what happens next.

1

Booking & Registration

The journey starts with the essentials: pet details, pickup and destination locations, timing, and contact information. You get a confirmation with a booking reference right away.

2

Travel Requirements Review

Before anything is scheduled, we check vaccination records, health certificates, crate suitability, and identification, so there's nothing left to catch us by surprise on pickup day.

3

Transport Planning & Scheduling

We confirm whether the trip is by air or by ground, coordinate the arrangements, and share an estimated timeframe so you know roughly when to expect updates.

4

Transport & Monitoring

While your pet is in transit, every milestone — pickup, transfers, stops along the route — is logged, so the tracking page always reflects where things actually stand.

5

Arrival & Delivery

Once your pet reaches its destination and delivery is complete, the booking status is updated to Delivered — closing the loop on the journey.
